Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

Classic New Weather ("we," "us," or "our") operates classicnewweather.com. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights around it. We keep things simple because we collect very little.

What we collect

We collect basic page view analytics — which pages were visited and when. This data contains no personally identifying information. We do not collect your name, email address, or precise location. We do not use cookies for tracking.

How we use it

Page view data is used only to understand how people use the site so we can improve it. We do not sell, share, or monetize this data in any form.

Third-party services

We use a small number of third-party services to operate the site:

  • Netlify — hosts this website. Netlify may collect standard server logs including IP addresses. See Netlify's privacy policy.
  • Supabase — stores our application data securely. See Supabase's privacy policy.
  • Meta / Instagram — we publish weather content to Instagram via the Meta API. We do not share any visitor data with Meta. See Meta's privacy policy.

Data retention

Page view analytics are retained for up to 12 months and then deleted.

Your rights

Since we don't collect personal information, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete. If you have questions, reach out and we'll do our best to help.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we make meaningful changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at info@classicnewwave.com.
